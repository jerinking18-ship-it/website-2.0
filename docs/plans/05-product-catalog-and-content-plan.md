# Product Catalog And Content Plan

## Starter Product Categories

- Fresh Produce
- Dairy And Eggs
- Bakery
- Pantry
- Beverages
- Frozen
- Household
- Organic

## Starter Product Fields

Each product should include:

- ID
- Name
- Category
- Price
- Original price when discounted
- Unit size
- Image or visual treatment
- Tags
- Rating
- Stock status
- Delivery eligibility

## Example Products

- Organic Bananas
- Roma Tomatoes
- Hass Avocados
- Farm Eggs
- Whole Milk
- Sourdough Bread
- Greek Yogurt
- Brown Rice
- Cold Brew Coffee
- Sparkling Water
- Frozen Berries
- Olive Oil

## Content Tone

Use clear customer-first copy:

- Short labels.
- Useful product details.
- No exaggerated claims.
- No filler marketing lines.

## Client Content Needed Later

- Final business name.
- Logo.
- Brand colors.
- Real product categories.
- Real product list and pricing.
- Delivery areas.
- Support email and phone number.
- Refund and freshness policy.
- Payment methods.
- Legal pages.
